Self-hosted Services

Goal

My goal is to self host my own services to have more control over my data and to learn more about networking and server management. I also want to be able to access my services from anywhere in the world without relying on third-party services.

Services

  • PBX
  • Automation
  • Tunnel
  • Local Password Database
  • Home Assistant
  • Dashboard

Design

  • AI Servers — llama3.2:3b, llava, nomic-embed-text, qwen2.5:7b
  • PBX — 3cx, with US number
  • Automation — n8n
  • Tunnel — Cloudflare
  • Local Password Database — Vaultwardem

Current status

  • PBX

    • I've configured 3cx to work in my local network; I want to configure it to work from outside my network such as with our phones data through the 3cx app; I also want to get it working with home assistant so i can call and talk to it; I also want to get it to have options on who to talk to.

  • AI

    • I want to configure it to act like an alexa in my home network. Ive currently set up speak to text and i'm figuring that out. Future projects will be setting up mics throughout the house and implementing it into home assistant

  • Tunnel

    • I have configured cloudflare tunnel so I can access my local services from the internet through an access wall.

  • Dashboard

    • I have configured Homarr as my dashboard to display all my self-hosted services this is also behind the access wall.

  • Home Assistant

    • I have configured Home Assistant OS on a container on my proxmox server and have it running on my local network. I have also configured it to work with my smart devices in my home. In the future, I plan to integrate it further with my PBX and AI server.

  • Automation

    • I have just started to learn about automation in n8n, in the future, I plan to integrate it further with my PBX and AI server.

  • Local Password Database

    • I have configured Vaultwarden as my local password database to store and manage my passwords securely.

  • Future Plans

    • In the future, I plan to integrate all my self-hosted services together to create a seamless and efficient home network. I also plan to explore more self-hosted services and implement them into my home network.

What I learned

  • I have learned how to configure and manage self-hosted services on my local network.
  • I have learned how to integrate different self-hosted services together to create a seamless and efficient home network.
  • I have learned how to troubleshoot issues that may arise when managing self-hosted services.
